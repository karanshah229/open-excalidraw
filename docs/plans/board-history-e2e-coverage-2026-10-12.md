## Production-only evidence and practical release conclusion

Real Firestore composite-index readiness, deployed security rules/App Check/auth, scheduler IAM/delivery/retries, GCS generation behavior at production latency, cleanup throughput/cost/backlog alerts, coordinated migration release/rollback, and long multi-editor soak tests require staging evidence. Emulator success cannot establish those properties.

The current suites provide meaningful normal, offline, guest/viewer, image, complete-tuple, fencing and recovery validation. They do not implement the entire ADR matrix. Prioritize exact timer edges, saves during multipage traversal and broader all-version fixtures, subsystem outage separation, multi-tab outbox ownership, cleanup crash/concurrency/backlog, and role/delete policy races before treating the broader release criteria as satisfied. Exceptional asset quarantine repair and cleanup observability remain implementation/rollout work described in the ADR appendix.
